{{ .Values.replicaCount }} replicas of the airport group are starting as
{{ include "airport-simulator.fullname" . }}-0 to -{{ sub (int .Values.replicaCount) 1 }}.

Watch them elect a leader:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout status statefulset/{{ include "airport-simulator.fullname" . }}

Then, from inside the cluster, send a command (a follower answers 307 with
the leader's address, which `curl -L` follows):

  curl -L -X POST http://{{ include "airport-simulator.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc:{{ .Values.service.port }}/commands \
    -d '{"client":1,"seq":1,"command":{"type":"advance_time","now_ms":1000}}'

GET /status shows a replica's role, term and state fingerprint; GET /snapshot
shows its airport.

WARNING: persistence is off. A replica that restarts forgets its log and its
vote, and the group can then lose commands it has already acknowledged.
{{- end }}
